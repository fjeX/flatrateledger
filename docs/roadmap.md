# Where it's going

Flat Rate Ledger starts as a technician's own record. Where it's going is something no single record can give you.

## True Time: the data techs have never had

Every job a tech times becomes an anonymous data point: how long the work really took, next to what it paid. Pooled across many technicians, that answers a question nobody can answer today: *how long does this job actually take?*

Warranty and flat-rate times aren't a perfect measure of a job, but they're usually well under what the same job is quoted at the counter. Real numbers from real techs put that on the record.

- **Live now:** collection. Opt-in, private, and only measured times count.
- **Next:** the shared view, once enough techs contribute. A figure appears only when at least five different techs are behind it.

## Closer to the paystub

- **Scan the paystub's RO list.** Photograph the list of ROs on a paystub and line it up against your own record automatically, instead of checking line by line. The review screens are built; it ships once it's proven on real paystubs.

## Growing the user base

The app is in a free beta with technicians I know. The next round opens it to more techs, and their feedback decides what gets built next, the same way the first features came from my own shop.
